Delete your TravAcs account

You can permanently delete your TravAcs account from the mobile application.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to TravAcs with your registered mobile number.
  2. Open the navigation menu.
  3. Select Delete account, immediately after Sign out.
  4. Read the warning and select Delete account again to confirm.

If you have an active trip, complete or cancel it before deleting your account.

Data removed

Successful deletion removes your sign-in account, profile and contact information, and notification device tokens.

Data retained

Historical trip, payment, accounting, and safety records may be retained where needed for operational, legal, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. Personal names, phone numbers, and gender copied into retained trip records are anonymized when the account is deleted.

If you cannot access the app

Email support@travacs.in with the subject TravAcs account deletion request. We will verify account ownership before processing the request.

Contact

Questions about account deletion can be sent to support@travacs.in.
